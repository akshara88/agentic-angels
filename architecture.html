Specialist Swarm — Investment Committee Architecture

A Chair (coordinator) + 7 specialist sub-agents + custom Skills, on Claude Managed Agents (multi-agent, beta managed-agents-2026-04-01). A founder pitch + third-party research goes in; a decision memo (outputs/ic-decision.md) comes out.

Chair / coordinator Due-diligence lens Risk (adversarial) Critic Custom Skill Synthetic data

0 · Provisioning pipeline (run once, in order)

setup_environment.py
→ .environment_id → create_specialists.py
→ .specialist_ids.json (7) → create_coordinator.py
→ .coordinator_id → upload_skills.py
→ .skill_ids.json → run_deal_desk.py
the swarm

Each script talks to the Anthropic Beta API (environments, agents, skills, sessions, files) and persists IDs to dotfiles that the next stage reads. upload_skills.py runs after create_coordinator.py because the Chair itself gets a skill (ic-memo-standard).

4 · Stage 2 — Risk Specialist (adversarial cross-examination)

Risk Specialist claude-opus-4-7
gathers no new evidence · cannot see the Chair's conversation
Cross-examines the five reports for CONTRADICTIONS, BLIND SPOTS, UNSUPPORTED CONFIDENCE, and the one thing that kills the deal. Surfaces conflicts — never resolves them. Ends with BLOCKERS / TOP RISK.
▼  Stage 3 — Chair drafts memo (no delegation) using ic-memo-standard
▼  Stage 4 — six reports + draft pasted into Critic's brief

5 · Stage 4 — IC Critic (review loop, max 2 revisions)

IC Critic claude-opus-4-7
leads with VERDICT: SHIP IT | REVISE | STOP
Checks the draft against the reports: bland consensus, missing ## Tensions, stripped UNVERIFIED tags, wrong weighting arithmetic, unsourced figures, ignored blockers. run_deal_desk.py surfaces the verdict live on the event stream. STOP → memo finalises as RECOMMENDATION: PASS, not abandoned.

6 · Output

Chair writes outputs/ic-decision.md
Every path ends in a written memo — SHIP IT, REVISE-twice, or STOP. run_deal_desk.py streams events live (the demo), saves coordinator-transcript.txt, then downloads every file the container produced into outputs/. download_deliverable.py re-fetches files from any past session by ID.
