## The ask

Raising **$6.0M seed** at **$30M post-money**. Lead sought for $4M.

## What we do

Ambient clinical documentation for **outpatient specialty clinics**. Our system
listens to the clinical encounter and produces a structured, billable note in the
clinic's EHR before the clinician leaves the room. We are built specifically for
specialty workflows — rheumatology, endocrinology, gastroenterology — not for the
general primary-care visit that the large vendors target.

## Product

Our note accuracy is **97.2%** measured against clinician-corrected ground truth
on a 2,000-encounter internal benchmark. We believe this is best-in-class.

Differentiators:
- **Specialty-tuned models.** We fine-tune per specialty rather than shipping one
  general model. Nobody else does this at our depth.
- **Sub-4-second note generation.** Fastest in the category.
- **Direct EHR write-back** into the three EHRs that dominate specialty practice,
  with no middleware.
- **Proprietary encounter corpus.** 2.1M de-identified specialty encounters, which
  improves the model in a way a horizontal competitor cannot replicate.

The demo is the sales cycle. In 27 of our last 31 demos, the clinician asked how
soon they could start. We convert demos at a rate we have not seen published
anywhere else.

## Market

The global healthcare AI market will exceed **$40B** by 2030. Clinical
documentation is the largest single application segment within it. We are
targeting the specialty slice, which is underserved by the horizontal players.
