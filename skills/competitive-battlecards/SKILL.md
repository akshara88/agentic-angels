# Competitive Battlecards

## Count the funded field first

Before assessing differentiation, state the shape of the field from the
competitor landscape document:

- How many funded players are selling to the same buyer today.
- Total capital raised across them, and the largest single war chest.
- How that compares to the round under consideration.

A company raising $6M into a field where the leader has raised $150M is not
necessarily uninvestable, but the memo must say the number out loud. Capital
asymmetry sets how long the company can afford to be undifferentiated.

**When the Market Specialist reports a large TAM and this document shows a
crowded funded field, those are not contradictory - they are the tension.** A big
market attracts capital. Say both, in the same breath, and let the Chair carry
the conflict into the memo. Do not resolve it by softening either side.

## Test the differentiation claim

For each claimed differentiator, assign one of three verdicts and justify it:

| Verdict | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Durable** | Compounds with usage or is structurally hard to copy - proprietary data that improves the product, switching costs, regulatory position, distribution lock |
| **Temporary** | A real lead today that a funded competitor can close in 2-4 quarters - model quality, UX polish, a feature |
| **Table stakes** | Every incumbent already has it; claiming it as a differentiator is a signal about the founders' market awareness |

Accuracy percentages, latency numbers, and model choice are almost always
Temporary. Say so, and note that being right about it today still matters -
Temporary advantages win deals in the current sales cycle.

## Assess by position, not by logo

For the two or three most relevant competitors:
1. Where they beat this company on this specific buyer's decision criteria.
2. Where this company beats them.
3. What each would do if this company started winning - the competitive response,
   priced and timed.

## The trap to avoid

Do not confuse an absent competitor with an unserved market. If nobody funded is
selling to a segment, ask whether the segment cannot buy. Note it explicitly when
the whitespace looks like a graveyard rather than an opening.

## Scoring

Score defensibility over a three-year horizon, not today's feature comparison.

| Score | Means |
| --- | --- |
| 9-10 | At least one Durable differentiator, and capital position to defend it |
| 7-8 | Credible path to Durable, currently leading on Temporary |
| 5-6 | Temporary advantages only, field is funded but not consolidated |
| 3-4 | Table-stakes claims, heavily funded field, no path to durable advantage |
| 1-2 | Structurally outgunned on capital and distribution |

## Output

Max 300 words, one message, no web search. Use only the documents provided.
Close with exactly:

```
SCORE: n/10
CONFIDENCE: high|medium|low
TOP RISK: <one line>
```
